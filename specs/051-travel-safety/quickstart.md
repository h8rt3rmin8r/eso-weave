# Quickstart

1. Install addon contract version 18.
2. Launch ESO Weave before ESO and confirm Travel is `Unknown`.
3. Enter the world and confirm Travel becomes `Inactive`.
4. Begin each supported travel mode and confirm Travel becomes `Pending` before loading.
5. Confirm generated weave, fishing, and potion input is suppressed while pending.
6. Cancel or fail travel and confirm safe recovery without replay.
7. Complete travel and confirm lifecycle recovery after activation.
8. Repeat for paid recall, free wayshrine, group, guild, house, door, cancellation, failure, and successful load.
