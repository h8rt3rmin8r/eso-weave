# Quickstart

1. Install addon contract version 19 and use keyboard UI mode.
2. Enter the world on foot and confirm Movement shows `On foot` while stationary and running.
3. Sprint for longer than 200 ms and confirm Movement becomes `Sprinting`.
4. Stop, swim, fall, roll dodge, die, load, and swap bars; confirm sprint clears without flapping.
5. Enable auto-potion with a resource below threshold and confirm no quickslot input occurs while sprinting.
6. Stop sprinting while the resource remains low and confirm one normal eligible potion attempt occurs.
7. Recover the resource or introduce another blocker before sprint ends and confirm no stale attempt fires.
8. Repeat in gamepad mode and while mounted; confirm no explicit sprint or gallop claim is published.
9. Exercise the complete live matrix in `research.md` before release validation is signed off.
