# Native Density Contract

## Precise-pointer comfortable profile

- Minimum normal control size: 28 by 28 logical points.
- Global item spacing: 8 logical points horizontally and 2 vertically.
- Button padding: 8 logical points horizontally and 4 vertically.
- Scaled body text may increase control height when required to avoid clipping.

## Conservative profile

- Touch-capable, coarse, mixed, or unknown pointer consumers retain a minimum 44 by 44 logical-point interaction target.
- ESO Weave does not claim this profile until it exposes and validates the necessary runtime capabilities.

## Preserved behavior

- Theme palette, focus width, radii, typography, status roles, and hover-stable geometry remain unchanged.
- Resource meters and explicit local widget sizes remain product-owned.
- No input, automation, addon, capture, storage, network, or privacy behavior changes.
